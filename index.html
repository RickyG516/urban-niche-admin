Outreach Sequences

Multi-touch DM, Email, and Cold Call playbooks

📱 DM Sequence — LinkedIn & Facebook
3-touch social media outreach. Conversational, not salesy. Goal: earn a reply, not close on touch 1.
Day 1 — Touch 1
The Opener: Connect + casual friendly intro. Zero pitch. Start the relationship.
Day 3–4 — Touch 2
The Value Drop: Share something useful. Quick win tip or relevant insight. Position as resource.
Day 7–10 — Touch 3
The Soft Ask: Light CTA. Ask if they're open to a quick conversation — no pressure.
📄 Open DM Sequence Doc
📧 Email Sequence — 5-Touch Cold
Under 150 words per email. Mobile-first. One CTA per email. Never pitch on email 1.
Day 1 — Email 1
Pattern Interrupt: Short and punchy. Identify what they're missing without being salesy.
Day 3 — Email 2
Proof Drop: Social proof or a relevant result. Make it real and specific.
Day 7 — Email 3
Value Add: Give them something free and useful. No strings attached.
Day 14 — Email 4
Follow-Up: "Did you get a chance to see this?" Simple bump, nothing more.
Day 21 — Email 5
The Breakup: Last email. Leave the door wide open. No hard feelings.

How To Use This Platform

Full operating manual — everything you need to run UNC outreach from this dashboard

⚡ Platform Overview▼

The UNC Command Center is your all-in-one outreach operating system for Urban Niche Co. It connects to a live Supabase database so everything you do — stage changes, notes, checklist ticks — saves in real time and is visible across all logged-in reps.

💡 Think of this as your war room. Every lead, every touchpoint, every action flows through here. Your Cowork agents do the execution — this platform tracks and directs them.
1
Add leads to your Pipeline — manually or via CSV import
2
Open a lead, run a Quick Win Audit via Cowork, send the cold outreach
3
Tick the checklist as you complete each touchpoint
4
Move the stage forward as they respond
5
Close the deal → set MRR → watch Pipeline MRR climb
🎯 Pipeline Tab▼

Your main workspace. Every lead assigned to you lives here. The Pipeline tab shows your KPIs, funnel visualization, hit list, and the full leads table with search, filter, and sort.

Adding Leads
1
Click + Add Lead or press N from anywhere in the pipeline

3
Set initial stage if they're already partway through (default: NEW)

Press Enter or click Add Lead — appears instantly in your table
CSV Import

Click 📤 CSV Import to bulk-load leads from a spreadsheet. Required column: company. Optional: city, state, industry, phone, website, notes. Duplicate company names are automatically skipped. File imports in chunks of 50 — handles hundreds of leads without issue.

Search, Filter & Quick Filters

Sorting

Click Company or Last Touched column headers to sort. Click again to reverse direction. Sort stale leads to the top by clicking Last Touched ↑ — these are your priority calls.

Today's Hit List

The Hit List shows your top 6 most-stale active leads in priority order. These are the ones most likely going cold — your first outreach priority every day. Appears on both the Pipeline tab and the Cadence tab.

📋 Lead Detail Panel▼

Click any lead row to open the detail panel. This is your per-lead command center — update everything about this contact without leaving the page.

Contact Info (Editable)

Owner name, phone, and website are all inline-editable directly in the panel. Click the field, type, and hit Save. Updates Supabase immediately. Phone becomes a clickable call link. Website becomes a clickable visit link in the table row.

Changing Stage

Use the Stage dropdown to move the lead forward. This saves to the database instantly, refreshes the stage badge in the panel header, updates the funnel and KPIs in the background, and logs the change to the Activity feed. Always move the stage as soon as you complete an action — this is how Last Touched stays current.

MRR Value

Set the monthly recurring revenue value for this lead once they're in PROPOSAL or CLOSED_WON. This feeds the Pipeline MRR KPI at the top of your dashboard. Even setting an estimated MRR on PROPOSAL leads helps you track real potential, not just wishful counting.

Outreach Checklist

Seven checkboxes that track every outreach touchpoint for this lead. Tick them off as you send each piece. Each tick logs to Activity, updates Last Touched, and keeps your history clean.

👥 Admin Tabs (Reps, Board, Activity, Pool) ADMIN▼

Admin-only tabs visible only to ricky.garnerfgc@gmail.com. Used to manage the team, monitor performance, and audit all activity.

Reps

View all registered users. Suspend or reactivate reps. Invite new reps via the Supabase Auth dashboard until SMTP email is configured (then the Invite Rep button will work directly).

Leaderboard (Board)

Weekly performance scores by rep. Pulls from the rep_metrics table — will populate automatically once the skill track scoring function is wired. Shows tier badges: Elite, Solid, On Notice, Performance Track.

Activity Feed

Last 100 logged actions across all reps in real time. Every stage change, checklist tick, notes save, and lead add is recorded here with the rep name, timestamp, and detail. Use this to audit what's happening and hold reps accountable.

Lead Pool

Unassigned leads — leads in the database with no assigned_to value. Reps can claim leads here. Admins can seed the pool via CSV import without assigning. Great for letting reps self-select their territory.

Assigning Leads to Reps
1
Go to Supabase SQL Editor or the leads table directly
2
Run: UPDATE leads SET assigned_to = '[rep_user_id]' WHERE city = 'Dubuque' — or use any filter
3
Or use CSV Import — include an assigned_to column with the rep's UUID to pre-assign on import
4
Or leave assigned_to blank on CSV import — leads land in the Pool tab and reps claim them
💡 Onboarding a new rep: 1. Invite via Supabase Auth → 2. Add their record to users table with role = 'rep' → 3. Bulk-import leads and leave assigned_to blank → 4. Rep claims from Pool tab.
🚀 How to Deploy Updates ADMIN▼

The platform deploys via GitHub Pages. Every push to the main branch auto-deploys in ~60 seconds. No build step, no server — just the HTML file.

🔥 Pro Tips & Daily Workflow▼
💡 Start every session: Hit the 🥶 Stale 7d+ quick filter first. Those leads are cooling fast. Work them before adding new ones.
💡 The one-per-day rule: Even one completed touchpoint per lead per day compounds fast. 10 leads × 1 touchpoint = 10 actions/day = 50/week.
💡 Never skip the checklist: The checklist IS your CRM. If it's not ticked, it didn't happen. This feeds the Activity log and your skill score.
💡 Notes are gold: Write the owner's name, anything personal they mentioned, their main pain point. When they finally reply weeks later, you'll know exactly who they are.
💡 Set MRR early: Put an estimated MRR even on PROPOSAL leads. Your Pipeline MRR shows what you're actually working toward, not just what's closed.
💡 Cowork is your leverage: Every Quick Win Audit takes 2 minutes via Cowork. That's your DM hook, your credibility, and your reason to reach out — all generated for free. Use it on every cold lead before first contact.
